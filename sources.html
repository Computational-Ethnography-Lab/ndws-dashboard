Sources and methods

Assisted Living releases

  • NDWS Assisted Living Staff, Wave 1 — E229523V3: 447 respondents, 35 facilities.
  • NDWS Assisted Living Staff, Wave 2 — E251473V1: 1,559 respondents, 120 facilities.

Maust, Spetz, Wagner, Marcus and Wagner (2026). National Dementia Workforce Study: Assisted Living Staff, Wave 2 (2025). ICPSR. Follow each DOI for its complete recommended citation. Data and derived results: CC BY-SA 4.0.

Interpretation

  • Each release uses its own FinalWeight. No pooling or substitute pooled weights.
  • Approximate 95% intervals use facility clusters; strata and finite population corrections are omitted.
  • Estimates with valid n < 30 are withheld before serialization; n = 30–49 is flagged. Complementary protection withholds additional totals and shares where needed so a withheld value cannot be recovered from published role, area or national totals.
  • Questionnaire and compensation charts use whole-wave eligible valid respondents. Area and role filters do not apply to those sections.
  • Sampling areas are not state estimates. Waves describe separate baseline samples, not within-person change.
  • The existing AL W2 model uses 1,508 complete cases in 120 facilities. In-sample weighted AUC: 0.824085; no held-out or causal claim.
  • Benefit availability is distinct from enrollment. Hourly pay bands are not converted to annual income.

Nursing Home and Community Clinician releases

  • NDWS Nursing Home Staff, Wave 1 — E234042V2
  • NDWS Nursing Home Staff, Wave 2 — E251478V1
  • NDWS Community Clinicians, Wave 1 Final — E232121V3

Each NH wave uses its own FinalWeight and facility clusters. CC is an individual-unit design with no facility cluster or stratum in the public file. The disclosure rules above apply to both. See NH and CC for page-specific notes.

How to read the methods

  • FinalWeight: the released survey weight accounts for selection and survey adjustments. A weighted percentage divides the weighted responses by the sum of weights among valid responses. A weighted total is the sum of these weights, not the number interviewed; do not add totals across waves.
  • Stratum: a group defined before sampling to organize sample selection. The present intervals do not incorporate stratification.
  • Facility group: NH groups combine bed-size and rural sampling strata. Small, mid-size and large groups are non-rural; rural facilities are grouped across sizes. These are not states or Census regions.
  • Facility cluster: workers in the same facility may have correlated responses. Staff intervals account for facility clustering; estimates supported by only one facility are withheld.
  • Finite population correction (FPC): an adjustment for sampling a substantial fraction of a finite population without replacement. It is not applied here.
  • Small samples: 30–49 valid responses may be displayed with a warning. Fewer than 30 are withheld. A zero observed category is different from a withheld estimate; a degenerate interval does not imply zero population uncertainty.
  • CC uses respondent-level intervals, not facility clusters. The public file does not provide facility or stratum identifiers.
