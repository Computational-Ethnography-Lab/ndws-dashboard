Community Clinicians

Community Clinician survey · Wave 1 final public-use file (V3) · 6,554 respondents

Design. Clinicians were sampled as individuals. The public file has no facility cluster or stratum, so intervals treat respondents as the sampling unit and are approximate. There are no facility groups, geography, model or map for this survey. Referral questions ask how often, not how many.
